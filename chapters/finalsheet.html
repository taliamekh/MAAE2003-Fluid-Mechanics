    📄 Page 12 — Properties · Hydrostatics · Flow Fundamentals

    Viscosity & specific weight
    P12Ch 1Shear stress & viscosity
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{\tau}=\Big(\tfrac{\text{tangential force}}{\text{surface area}}\Big)=\lim_{\Delta A\to0}\frac{\Delta \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_{t}}{\Delta \textcolor{#1565C0}{A}} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{\tau}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\mu}\,\frac{d\textcolor{#E65100}{V}}{d\textcolor{#1565C0}{y}}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\mu}\,\frac{\Delta \textcolor{#E65100}{V}}{\Delta \textcolor{#1565C0}{y}} \]

    Used for: the frictional shear a fluid exerts; linear velocity profile between a moving and stationary wall.

    P12Ch 1Specific weight
    \[ \textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g} \]

    Used for: weight per unit volume. ρ = fluid density (kg/m³); γ = specific weight (N/m³).

    Pressure & specific gravity
    P12Ch 1Pressure & gauge pressure
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}=\frac{\text{magnitude of normal force}}{\text{unit area}}=\lim_{\Delta A\to0}\frac{|\Delta \vec{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}}_{n}|}{\Delta \textcolor{#1565C0}{A}} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{\text{gage}}=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{\text{abs}}-\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{\text{atm}} \]

    Used for: pressure at a point in a fluid; converting absolute ↔ gauge.

    P12Ch 1Specific gravity
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{SG}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}/\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}_{\text{water}}\quad(\text{liquid}) \]
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{SG}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}/\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}_{\text{air}}\quad(\text{gas}) \]

    Used for: density relative to water (liquids) or air (gases). Recover ρ = SG × ρ_ref.

    Hydrostatic pressure variation
    P12Ch 1Incompressible Δp with depth
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_2-\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_1=-\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\,(\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_2-\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_1) \]

    Used for: pressure change between two depths in a static incompressible fluid.

    P12Ch 1Manometer (multiple liquids)
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_2=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_1+\sum_{\text{down}}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}_i \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_i-\sum_{\text{up}}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}_i \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_i \]

    Used for: tracing pressure through a manometer: +γh going down, −γh going up, switch γ at each interface.

    Forces on a flat (plane) surface
    P12Ch 2Resultant force on flat surface
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}=(\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_a+\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{CG})\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{CG}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{A} \]

    Used for: total hydrostatic force = pressure at the centroid × area.

    P12Ch 2Centre of pressure
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{y}_{CP}=-\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\sin\textcolor{#7B1FA2}{\theta}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{I}_{xx}}{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{CG}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}} \]
    \[ \text{open to atm:}\quad \textcolor{#1565C0}{y}_{CP}=-\frac{\textcolor{#1565C0}{I}_{xx}\sin\textcolor{#7B1FA2}{\theta}}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{CG}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}} \]

    Used for: distance of the centre of pressure below the centroid (always below).

    Forces on curved surfaces
    P12Ch 2Curved surface F_V, F_H
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_V=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_1+\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_2+\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_{\text{air}} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{x}_{CP}=\frac{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_1 \textcolor{#1565C0}{x}_1+\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_2 \textcolor{#1565C0}{x}_2+\textcolor{#D32F2F}{W}_{\text{air}} \textcolor{#1565C0}{x}_{\text{air}}}{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_V} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_H=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{CG}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}_{ac} \]

    Used for: vertical force = weight of fluid above; horizontal force = flat-plate force on the projection.

    P12Ch 2Curved surface y_CP, z_CP
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{y}_{CP}=-\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{I}_{xx}}{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_{CG}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}_{ac}} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_{CP}=\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_{CG}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{y}_{CP} \]

    Used for: locating the horizontal-force line of action on a curved surface.

    Buoyancy & stability
    P12Ch 2Buoyancy
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_B=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}_{\text{fluid}}\times\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}_{\text{displaced}} \]

    Used for: upward buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid (Archimedes).

    P12Ch 2Metacentric height
    \[ \overline{\textcolor{#1565C0}{MG}}=\frac{\textcolor{#1565C0}{I}_O}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}_{\text{sub}}}-\overline{\textcolor{#1565C0}{GB}} \]

    Used for: floating-body stability; MG > 0 ⇒ stable. I_O = waterline-area second moment.

    Rigid-body motion (acceleration effects on pressure)
    P12§2.9Horizontal acceleration
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}\qquad\&\qquad\tan\textcolor{#7B1FA2}{\theta}=\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{a}_x}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}} \]

    Used for: tilt of a free surface under constant horizontal acceleration.

    P12§2.9Rotational acceleration
    \[ \textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}=\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_0-\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}+\tfrac12\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{r}^2\textcolor{#7B1FA2}{\Omega}^2 \]

    Used for: pressure field in a rotating (rigid-body) fluid; paraboloid free surface.

    Flow rates · RTT · continuity · Bernoulli
    P12Ch 5Volume & mass flow rate
    \[ \textcolor{#E65100}{Q}=\frac{d\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}}{dt}=\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}\cdot \textcolor{#E65100}{v} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}=\frac{d\textcolor{#1565C0}{m}}{dt}=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#E65100}{Q} \]

    Used for: the two flow rates; Q = AV sets the velocity every pipe formula needs.

    P12Ch 5Reynolds transport theorem
    \[ \frac{d}{dt}(B_{\text{sys}})=\frac{d}{dt}\!\Big(\!\int_{CV}\textcolor{#00838F}{\beta}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}\Big)+\int_{CS}\textcolor{#00838F}{\beta}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_r\!\cdot\! \vec n)\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{A} \]

    Used for: converting a system law into a control-volume statement (β = intensive property).

    P12Ch 5Conservation of mass
    \[ \text{General:}\quad\frac{d}{dt}\!\Big(\!\int_{CV}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}\Big)+\int_{CS}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}\!\cdot\! \vec n)\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}=0 \]
    \[ \text{1-D:}\quad\frac{d}{dt}\!\Big(\!\int_{CV}\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}\Big)+\sum \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}}-\sum \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}}=0 \]

    Used for: mass balance; steady ⇒ Σṁ_out = Σṁ_in.

    P12Ch 3Bernoulli's equation
    \[ \frac{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_1}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}}+\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_1=\frac{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_2}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}}+\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_2=\text{const} \]

    Used for: steady, incompressible, frictionless flow along a streamline (no losses, no machine).

    📄 Page 13 — Energy · Momentum · Pipe Flow

    Energy equation
    P13Ch 5Energy equation (integral form)
    \[ \dot Q-\dot W_{\text{shaft}}=\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Big[\int_{CV}\big(\hat u+\tfrac12 \textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2+\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}\big)\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,d\textcolor{#1565C0}{\forall}\Big]+\sum\big(\hat h+\tfrac12 \textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2+\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}\big)_{\text{out}}\textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}}-\sum\big(\hat h+\tfrac12 \textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2+\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}\big)_{\text{in}}\textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}} \]

    Used for: first law on a CV. Storage uses internal energy û; flux uses enthalpy ĥ = û + p/ρ.

    Specific energy (head) equation & α
    P13Ch 5Head equation (steady)
    \[ \Big(\frac{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_1}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}}+\textcolor{#00838F}{\alpha}_1\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_1\Big)=\Big(\frac{\textcolor{#D32F2F}{p}_2}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\gamma}}+\textcolor{#00838F}{\alpha}_2\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{z}_2\Big)+\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{loss}}-\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{pump}}+\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{turbine}} \]

    Used for: Bernoulli with losses + machines. Sign pattern: +loss, −pump, +turbine. The master pipe-flow equation.

    P13Ch 5KE correction factor α
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{\alpha}=\frac{1}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{A}}\int\Big(\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{u}}{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_{av}}\Big)^3 d\textcolor{#1565C0}{A} \]

    Used for: correcting kinetic energy in the head equation. α ≈ 2 (laminar), ≈ 1.05 (turbulent).

    Pump & turbine power and efficiency
    P13Ch 5Pump power & efficiency
    \[ \dot W_f=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#E65100}{Q}\,\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{pump}} \qquad \textcolor{#00838F}{\eta}_{\text{pump}}=\frac{\dot W_f}{\dot W_{\text{pump}}} \]

    Used for: hydraulic power a pump adds; efficiency = useful fluid power ÷ shaft power.

    P13Ch 5Turbine power & efficiency
    \[ \dot W_f=\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#E65100}{Q}\,\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{turbine}} \qquad \textcolor{#00838F}{\eta}_{\text{turbine}}=\frac{\dot W_{\text{turbine}}}{\dot W_f} \]

    Used for: power a turbine extracts; efficiency flips vs a pump (shaft ÷ fluid).

    Linear momentum
    P13Ch 5Momentum, fixed CV
    \[ \sum \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_x=-\sum(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_{\text{in}})_x \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}}+\sum(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_{\text{out}})_x \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}} \]
    \[ \sum \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_y=-\sum(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_{\text{in}})_y \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}}+\sum(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_{\text{out}})_y \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}} \]

    Used for: forces on bends / nozzles / gates. Pick +x/+y, sum momentum flux, solve for the reaction.

    P13Ch 5Momentum, accelerating CV
    \[ \sum \textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}_{\text{ext}}=\textcolor{#1565C0}{m}\,\vec{\textcolor{#E65100}{a}}_{CV}-\sum \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}}\vec{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}}_{f/CV,\text{in}}+\sum \textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}}\vec{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}}_{f/CV,\text{out}} \]

    Used for: rockets / accelerating control volumes; uses relative velocities V_{f/CV}.

    Propellers & turbines (Froude) · angular momentum
    P13Ch 5Froude / propeller & turbine
    \[ \textcolor{#E65100}{V}=\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1+\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2}{2} \]
    \[ \text{thrust:}\;\textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}=\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\pi \textcolor{#1565C0}{R}^2}{2}(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2^2-\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1^2),\;\; \textcolor{#00838F}{\eta}_{\text{prop}}=\frac{2\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1}{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1+\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2} \]
    \[ \text{turbine:}\;\textcolor{#D32F2F}{F}=\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\pi \textcolor{#1565C0}{R}^2}{2}(\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1^2-\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2^2),\;\; \textcolor{#00838F}{\eta}_{\text{turbine}}=\tfrac12\Big[1-\tfrac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2^2}{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1^2}\Big]\Big[1+\tfrac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_2}{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}_1}\Big] \]

    Used for: actuator-disk propeller / wind-turbine thrust, swept-disk velocity, ideal efficiencies.

    P13Ch 5Angular momentum, fixed CV
    \[ \sum \textcolor{#D32F2F}{M}_{\text{ext}}=-\sum(\vec{\textcolor{#1565C0}{r}}\times\vec{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}}_{\text{in}})\textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{in}}+\sum(\vec{\textcolor{#1565C0}{r}}\times\vec{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}}_{\text{out}})\textcolor{#E65100}{\dot m}_{\text{out}} \]

    Used for: torque on sprinklers / turbomachinery; moment arm r about the chosen axis.

    Reynolds number & major (friction) loss
    P13Ch 6Reynolds number
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d=\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\rho}\,\textcolor{#E65100}{V}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\mu}}=\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\nu}} \]

    Used for: the regime gate — laminar < 2300, turbulent > 4000. ν = μ/ρ.

    P13Ch 6Weisbach (major head loss)
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f=\textcolor{#00838F}{f}\,\frac{\textcolor{#1565C0}{L}}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}\,\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}} \]

    Used for: friction head loss down a straight pipe (Darcy–Weisbach). f = Darcy factor.

    Friction factor
    P13Ch 6Friction factor — laminar
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{f}_{\text{lam}}=\frac{64}{\textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d} \]

    Used for: laminar friction factor — exact, roughness-independent, no chart.

    P13Ch 6Friction factor — turbulent (Haaland)
    \[ \frac{1}{\textcolor{#00838F}{f}^{1/2}}\approx-1.8\log\Big[\frac{6.9}{\textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d}+\Big(\frac{\textcolor{#00838F}{\varepsilon}/\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}{3.7}\Big)^{1.11}\Big] \]

    Used for: turbulent friction factor (explicit, Haaland). The ONLY turbulent-f equation on the sheet — no Colebrook; the Moody chart (p.14) is the graphical alternative.

    Pipe-flow problem types
    P13Ch 6Pipe-flow types (1 / 2 / 3)
    \[ \textbf{Type 1:}\;\; \textcolor{#1565C0}{d},\,\textcolor{#E65100}{Q}\;\Rightarrow\;\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f \qquad\qquad \textbf{Type 2:}\;\; \textcolor{#1565C0}{d},\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f\;\Rightarrow\;\textcolor{#E65100}{Q} \]
    \[ \textcolor{#00838F}{\zeta}=\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}^3 \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{L}\,\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\nu}^2},\quad \textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d=-(8\textcolor{#00838F}{\zeta})^{1/2}\log\Big(\frac{\textcolor{#00838F}{\varepsilon}/\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}{3.7}+\frac{1.775}{\sqrt{\textcolor{#00838F}{\zeta}}}\Big),\quad \textcolor{#E65100}{V}=\frac{\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\nu}\,\textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}} \]
    \[ \textbf{Type 3:}\;\; \textcolor{#E65100}{Q},\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f\;\Rightarrow\;\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}\,:\quad \textcolor{#1565C0}{d}^5=\frac{8\textcolor{#00838F}{f}\textcolor{#1565C0}{L}\textcolor{#E65100}{Q}^2}{\pi^2 \textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}\,\textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_f},\quad \textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d=\frac{4\textcolor{#E65100}{Q}}{\pi \textcolor{#1565C0}{d}\,\textcolor{#2E7D32}{\nu}},\quad \textcolor{#00838F}{f}=F\big(\textcolor{#00838F}{Re}_d,\textcolor{#00838F}{\varepsilon}/\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}\big) \]

    Used for: the three single-pipe problems — name the type by the missing variable (h_f → 1, Q → 2, d → 3).

    Minor & total losses
    P13Ch 6Minor loss
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_m=\textcolor{#00838F}{K}\,\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}} \]

    Used for: local loss at one fitting / valve / bend / entrance / exit. K from the table on p.14.

    P13Ch 6Total losses
    \[ \textcolor{#1565C0}{h}_{\text{total}}=\sum \textcolor{#00838F}{f}\,\frac{\textcolor{#1565C0}{L}}{\textcolor{#1565C0}{d}}\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}}+\sum \textcolor{#00838F}{K}\,\frac{\textcolor{#E65100}{V}^2}{2\textcolor{#2E7D32}{g}} \]

    Used for: total head loss = Σ major + Σ minor. Single-diameter pipe ⇒ factor out V²/2g.
