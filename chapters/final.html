🚫 Don't waste time on these

  • Memorising the Moody chart — you'll be given it on the sheet (or use Haaland). Practice reading it, don't redraw it.
  • Hand-deriving Colebrook or the laminar parabolic profile — only the result \(f=64/Re\) is used.
  • Compressible flow / Mach number — not in scope.
  • Re-deriving RTT or the generalized energy equation — apply the final forms.

💡 Insider Tips

  • Name the pipe problem by what's MISSING first. Missing \(h_f\) → Type 1 (direct march). Missing \(Q\) → Type 2 (explicit ζ). Missing \(d\) → Type 3 (iterate). A circuit with a pump or two reservoirs at different levels → complete-system.
  • Moody and Colebrook are turbulent-only. If \(Re_d<2300\), use \(f=64/Re_d\) — no chart. Check the regime before you reach for the friction factor.
  • Area changes use the smaller-pipe velocity. Sudden expansion / contraction \(K\) is applied to the faster (smaller-diameter) stream in \(K\,V^2/2g\).
  • Memorise the standard \(K\) values. Sharp entrance \(\approx 0.5\); sharp exit \(=1.0\) (the whole velocity head is dumped); open globe valve \(\approx 5\text{–}10\); gate valve \(\approx 0.1\text{–}0.2\).
  • \(\Delta p=\gamma h_f\) is horizontal & constant-area only. The moment there's an elevation change or a machine, go back to the full head equation.
  • Watch BG units. Many textbook pipe examples are in BG — keep \(g=32.2\ \text{ft/s}^2\) in the head terms and slugs straight.
  • State the regime and unit-check every pipe answer. "\(Re=\dots\Rightarrow\) turbulent" is often a marked step on its own.

🧭 Problem Type → Strategy

Problem looks like…Use…Key equation
Given \(d\) and \(Q\), find head loss / pressure dropType 1 rating (direct march)\(h_f=f\dfrac{L}{d}\dfrac{V^2}{2g}\), \(\;Re_d=\dfrac{Vd}{\nu}\), \(\;\Delta p=\gamma h_f\)
Given \(d\) and \(h_f\), find the flow rate \(Q\)Type 2 analysis (explicit ζ)\(\zeta=\dfrac{g\,d^3 h_f}{L\nu^2}\), \(\;Re_d=-\sqrt{8\zeta}\,\log\!\Big(\tfrac{\varepsilon/d}{3.7}+\tfrac{1.775}{\sqrt{\zeta}}\Big)\)
Given \(Q\) and \(h_f\), find the diameter \(d\)Type 3 design (iterate)\(d^5=\dfrac{8fLQ^2}{\pi^2 g\,h_f}\), \(\;Re_d=\dfrac{4Q}{\pi d\nu}\)
Pump moving fluid between two reservoirs / up a systemComplete system: head equation + all losses\(\dfrac{p_1}{\gamma}+\dfrac{V_1^2}{2g}+z_1=\dfrac{p_2}{\gamma}+\dfrac{V_2^2}{2g}+z_2+h_{loss}-h_p\), \(\;h_{loss}=\sum h_f+\sum h_m\)
Find the force to hold a bend / nozzle / elbowLinear momentum (RTT) + Bernoulli\(\sum\vec F=\sum_{\text{out}}\dot m\vec V-\sum_{\text{in}}\dot m\vec V\)
